本文1
Herb Pot Morning
The garden club will have Herb Pot Morning.

When: Saturday, May 16, 9:00–11:00 a.m.
Where: School Garden

We will plant basil and mint in small pots.
Please wear old clothes and bring a small towel.
After the event, you can take your pot home.

If you want to join, talk to Mr. Nomiya by May 12.

問題1a:
What will students plant at Herb Pot Morning?

選択肢1a:
1. Only mint.
2. Basil and mint.
3. Apple trees.
4. Old clothes.

【正解1a】
2. Basil and mint.

【解説1a】
正解は「Basil and mint.」です。
本文に「We will plant basil and mint in small pots.」とあります。植えるのはバジルとミントの両方です。
「Only mint」はミントだけになってしまうので違います。「Old clothes」は着るもの、「Apple trees」はこの案内にはありません。
ヒント：plant（植える）のあとに、作るもの・植えるものが続きます。wear（着る）と mix しないようにしましょう。

問題1b:
When should students talk to Mr. Nomiya to join?

選択肢1b:
1. On May 16 only.
2. After Herb Pot Morning.
3. By May 12.
4. Every Saturday in May.

【正解1b】
3. By May 12.

【解説1b】
正解は「By May 12.」です。
参加したい人は「talk to Mr. Nomiya by May 12」と書いてあります。by は「その日まで」という意味です。
5月16日は当日、毎週土曜という指定はありません。終わってからでは申し込みに間に合いません。
ヒント：申し込みの期限は by / before のあとの日付です。When: の行はイベント当日です。

本文2
From: Mei
To: Sho
Subject: Folding table

Hi Sho,

Can I borrow your family's folding table this weekend?
Our class will sell baked potatoes at the school festival on Sunday.
We need one more table because our desk is too small.

I will pick it up on Friday after school. Please bring it to the art room at 4:00 p.m.
I will take it home on Sunday night and bring it back to you on Monday morning.

Please put a red name card on the table. Many tables in the art room will look the same.
I do not want to take the wrong one.

If you cannot come on Friday, your brother can leave it by the art room window.

Can you send me a message tonight?

Thanks,
Mei

問題2a:
What will Mei's class sell at the festival?

選択肢2a:
1. Baked potatoes.
2. Red name cards.
3. Folding tables.
4. Apple juice.

【正解2a】
1. Baked potatoes.

【解説2a】
正解は「Baked potatoes.」です。
本文に「Our class will sell baked potatoes at the school festival on Sunday.」とあります。
赤い名札は机の印、折りたたみテーブルは借りる道具です。売るものではありません。アップルジュースの記述はありません。
ヒント：sell（売る）の直後が、模擬店の商品です。borrow（借りる）の目的語と混ぜないようにしましょう。

問題2b:
When will Mei bring the table back to Sho?

選択肢2b:
1. On Friday after school.
2. At 4:00 p.m. on Sunday.
3. On Sunday night.
4. On Monday morning.

【正解2b】
4. On Monday morning.

【解説2b】
正解は「On Monday morning.」です。
本文に「bring it back to you on Monday morning」とあります。
金曜の放課後4時は受け取り、日曜の夜はメイが家へ持って帰る時です。返す日ではありません。
ヒント：pick it up（受け取る）と bring it back（返す）の日付を並べて読むとまちがいません。

問題2c:
What can Sho's brother do if Sho cannot come on Friday?

選択肢2c:
1. Sell potatoes at the festival.
2. Leave the table by the art room window.
3. Take the table to Mei's house.
4. Make a red name card at school.

【正解2c】
2. Leave the table by the art room window.

【解説2c】
正解は「Leave the table by the art room window.」です。
「If you cannot come on Friday, your brother can leave it by the art room window.」とあります。
兄ができるのは、美術室の窓のそばにテーブルを置くことです。模擬店の手伝いや名札作りの話ではありません。
ヒント：If you cannot ... の文は、予定がだめなときの別のやり方です。

本文3
Last Monday, Yui had to draw the morning moon for science class.
She wanted a nice picture, so she set her clock for five o'clock.

On Tuesday morning, the sky was full of gray clouds. She opened the window, but she could not see the moon.
Yui sat down at her desk and almost cried.

Her older brother Kaito came into the room. He looked at the sky too.
He said, "Let's go to the library. There is a big book about the moon."
They walked to the library together before school and found the book near the window.
Yui copied a picture from the book. Under the picture she wrote, "I could not see the moon, so I used a book."

In science class, her teacher, Ms. Kibe, looked at the picture. She smiled and said, "You did not stop. That is good science."
Yui was happy, and she thanked Kaito on the way home.

問題3a:
What did Yui have to draw for science class?

選択肢3a:
1. Gray clouds.
2. Her older brother.
3. The morning moon.
4. The school library.

【正解3a】
3. The morning moon.

【解説3a】
正解は「The morning moon.」です。
最初の文に「Yui had to draw the morning moon for science class.」とあります。
雲は見えなかった理由、図書館はあとから行った場所です。兄を描く課題ではありません。
ヒント：物語の最初の文に、やらなければいけない宿題が書いてあることが多いです。

問題3b:
Why could Yui not see the moon?

選択肢3b:
1. Because the sky was full of gray clouds.
2. Because the library was closed.
3. Because her clock did not work.
4. Because Ms. Kibe took the book.

【正解3b】
1. Because the sky was full of gray clouds.

【解説3b】
正解は「Because the sky was full of gray clouds.」です。
「the sky was full of gray clouds」「she could not see the moon」と続いています。
時計は五時にセットできています。図書館はあとで開いていて、本も見つかります。
ヒント：but の前（空が曇っていた）が、見えなかった理由です。

問題3c:
Where did Yui and Kaito find the moon book?

選択肢3c:
1. On Yui's desk at home.
2. In the science classroom.
3. Under a shop roof.
4. Near the window in the library.

【正解3c】
4. Near the window in the library.

【解説3c】
正解は「Near the window in the library.」です。
「found the book near the window」は、二人で図書館へ行ったあとの文です。
家の机はユイが泣きそうになった場所、理科の教室は提出する場所です。
ヒント：同じ window でも、家の窓と図書館の窓は別です。前後の文で場所を確かめましょう。

問題3d:
What did Yui write under the picture?

選択肢3d:
1. "The library is closed today."
2. "I could not see the moon, so I used a book."
3. "Kaito drew this picture for me."
4. "Please look at the gray clouds."

【正解3d】
2. "I could not see the moon, so I used a book."

【解説3d】
正解は「I could not see the moon, so I used a book.」です。
本文に、絵の下にそう書いたとあります。兄が描いたわけでも、図書館が閉まっていたわけでもありません。
ヒント：引用符 " " の中は、登場人物が書いた・言った言葉そのものです。

問題3e:
Why did Ms. Kibe smile?

選択肢3e:
1. Because Yui saw the moon at five o'clock.
2. Because Kaito did the homework for Yui.
3. Because Yui did not stop.
4. Because the picture was from a TV show.

【正解3e】
3. Because Yui did not stop.

【解説3e】
正解は「Because Yui did not stop.」です。
先生は「You did not stop. That is good science.」と言ってほほえみました。途中であきらめなかったことがほめられています。
五時に月は見えていません。兄は本の場所を教えただけで、宿題を代わりにしてはいません。
ヒント：先生の発言の直後に、なぜほめたかが書いてあることが多いです。

本文4
Lunchtime Seed Share
The nature club will have a Seed Share Table.

When: Monday to Friday, 12:20–12:40
Where: By the science room door

You can take flower seeds and leave seeds from your garden.
Write your name on a small card.
Please do not run in the hallway.

Talk to Ms. Hara if you have questions.

問題4a:
Where is the Seed Share Table?

選択肢4a:
1. In the school garden.
2. In the library.
3. In the gym.
4. By the science room door.

【正解4a】
4. By the science room door.

【解説4a】
正解は「By the science room door.」です。
Where の行に「By the science room door」とあります。
花の種は庭から持ってきてもよい、という話であって、テーブルの場所が庭だとは書いてありません。図書館・体育館は出てきません。
ヒント：場所は Where: の行を先に見ると早いです。本文の garden は種の出所です。

問題4b:
What should students write on a small card?

選択肢4b:
1. Their name.
2. Ms. Hara's phone number.
3. The time of science class.
4. The color of the flowers.

【正解4b】
1. Their name.

【解説4b】
正解は「Their name.」です。
「Write your name on a small card.」とあります。
原田先生は質問があるときの相手で、電話番号は書かれていません。授業の時刻や花の色をカードに書く指示もありません。
ヒント：Write のあとに、カードへ書く内容が続きます。

本文5
From: Riku
To: Grandma Fumi
Subject: Flower stamps

Dear Grandma Fumi,

Thank you for the flower stamps. They came yesterday afternoon.
My class will make postcards next week, and I want to use your stamps.
We will send the cards to children in another town.

Can you send me more of the pink stamps? I still have the yellow ones, but we need many pink flowers.
Please put them in a small envelope. You can give them to Dad. He will visit you on Friday.

I will visit you next month. I can help you in the garden, and I can wash the bird cage too.
I want to hear your stories about the garden.

If it rains on Saturday, I will come on Sunday morning. I will bring cookies from the shop near the station.

Love,
Riku

問題5a:
What will Riku's class make next week?

選択肢5a:
1. A bird cage.
2. Postcards.
3. Pink cookies.
4. A garden map.

【正解5a】
2. Postcards.

【解説5a】
正解は「Postcards.」です。
「My class will make postcards next week」とあります。
鳥かごは来月お手伝いするときの話、クッキーはリクが持っていくおやつです。地図の記述はありません。
ヒント：class will make のあとが、クラスの作業です。I can の手伝いは別の段落です。

問題5b:
Which stamps does Riku want Grandma Fumi to send?

選択肢5b:
1. Only the yellow stamps.
2. Stamps with birds on them.
3. More of the pink stamps.
4. Stamps from the station shop.

【正解5b】
3. More of the pink stamps.

【解説5b】
正解は「More of the pink stamps.」です。
「Can you send me more of the pink stamps? I still have the yellow ones」とあります。黄色い切手はもう持っています。
鳥の切手や駅の店の切手は書かれていません。
ヒント：still have（まだ持っている）と more（もっとほしい）を対で読むと、色をまちがえません。

問題5c:
When will Riku visit if it rains on Saturday?

選択肢5c:
1. On Sunday morning.
2. On Friday with Dad.
3. Yesterday afternoon.
4. Next week before class.

【正解5c】
1. On Sunday morning.

【解説5c】
正解は「On Sunday morning.」です。
「If it rains on Saturday, I will come on Sunday morning.」とあります。
金曜はお父さんが切手を受け取りに行く日、yesterday は切手が届いた日です。
ヒント：If it rains の文は、雨のときの予定変更です。ほかの日付と役割を分けましょう。

本文6
Kota was walking home in the rain after buying bread at the bakery. He had a new blue umbrella from his father.

Near the station, a small first-grader was standing under a shop roof. She had no umbrella, and her bag was getting wet.
Kota said, "You can use this." He gave her his umbrella and told her his name and his school.

Kota ran home and got very wet. Water went into his shoes. His mother opened the door and looked surprised.
Kota told her about the girl near the station. His mother was not angry. She said, "You helped her. That was kind."

She gave Kota a dry shirt and a cup of hot milk. Then she put his wet shoes by the window.
The next morning, the girl came to school with Kota's umbrella and a small cookie. "Thank you," she said.
Kota smiled and took the umbrella. He gave the cookie to his mother after school.

問題6a:
When was Kota walking home?

選択肢6a:
1. Before he bought bread.
2. On Sunday morning.
3. After science class.
4. After buying bread at the bakery.

【正解6a】
4. After buying bread at the bakery.

【解説6a】
正解は「After buying bread at the bakery.」です。
最初の文に「walking home in the rain after buying bread at the bakery」とあります。パンを買う前ではありません。
日曜の朝や理科の授業はこの物語に出てこない時間です。
ヒント：物語の書き出しに、いつ・どこで・何のあとかがまとまっていることが多いです。

問題6b:
What did Kota give the first-grader?

選択肢6b:
1. A cup of hot milk.
2. His new blue umbrella.
3. His wet shoes.
4. A small cookie.

【正解6b】
2. His new blue umbrella.

【解説6b】
正解は「His new blue umbrella.」です。
「He gave her his umbrella」とあります。新しい青い傘は父からもらったものです。
ホットミルクと濡れた靴は家での話、クッキーは次の朝に女の子が持ってくるものです。
ヒント：gave her / gave him の目的語と、あとから別の人が渡すものを区別しましょう。

問題6c:
What did Kota's mother say about his action?

選択肢6c:
1. It was kind.
2. He should buy a new umbrella.
3. He must not walk home in the rain.
4. The first-grader was angry.

【正解6c】
1. It was kind.

【解説6c】
正解は「It was kind.」です。
お母さんは怒らず、「You helped her. That was kind.」と言いました。
新しい傘を買え、サッカーをするな、女の子が怒っている、といった記述はありません。
ヒント：looked surprised（驚いた顔）でも、次の文で怒っていないことが分かります。発言の中身を読みましょう。

問題6d:
What did the girl bring to school the next morning?

選択肢6d:
1. Hot milk and a dry shirt.
2. Kota's bakery bag.
3. Kota's umbrella and a small cookie.
4. Flower stamps in an envelope.

【正解6d】
3. Kota's umbrella and a small cookie.

【解説6d】
正解は「Kota's umbrella and a small cookie.」です。
「the girl came to school with Kota's umbrella and a small cookie」とあります。
シャツとミルクはお母さんがコタに出した夜の話です。サッカーバッグや切手は出ていません。
ヒント：the next morning の段落に、翌日の出来事がまとまっています。

問題6e:
What did Kota do with the cookie?

選択肢6e:
1. He ate it at the station.
2. He gave it to the first-grader again.
3. He put it in his wet shoes.
4. He gave it to his mother after school.

【正解6e】
4. He gave it to his mother after school.

【解説6e】
正解は「He gave it to his mother after school.」です。
最後の文に「He gave the cookie to his mother after school.」とあります。
駅で食べたり、靴に入れたり、女の子に返し直したりはしていません。
ヒント：物語の最後の文に、その人の気持ちや次の行動が書いてあることが多いです。
