No.1:
W: Next Sunday, Nao will work at the community garden near her house. She will water the tomato plants and pick weeds. She wants to take home some fresh herbs.

Question No.1:
Where will Nao work next Sunday?

1. At a community garden.
2. At a bakery.
3. At a museum.
4. At a library.

【正解1】
1. At a community garden.

【解説1】
放送文
W: Next Sunday, Nao will work at the community garden near her house.（次の日曜日、ナオは家の近くのコミュニティガーデンで働きます。）
She will water the tomato plants and pick weeds.（トマトに水をやり、雑草を抜きます。）
She wants to take home some fresh herbs.（新鮮なハーブを家に持ち帰りたいと思っています。）
Question: Where will Nao work next Sunday?（ナオは次の日曜日、どこで働きますか？）

「community garden」とあるので、1が正解です。
ハーブを持ち帰る話はあとでしたいことで、働く場所ではありません。

No.5:
W: Last Saturday, Ren practiced the guitar for two hours. Then he played a song for his grandparents online. They smiled and clapped at the end. His little sister sat beside him and listened.

Question No.5:
What did Ren do after practicing?

1. He went to a nearby concert hall.
2. He bought a new guitar downtown.
3. He slept in his room all day.
4. He played online for his grandparents.

【正解5】
4. He played online for his grandparents.

【解説5】
放送文
W: Last Saturday, Ren practiced the guitar for two hours.（先週の土曜日、レンは2時間ギターを練習しました。）
Then he played a song for his grandparents online.（そのあと、オンラインでおじいちゃん・おばあちゃんに曲を演奏しました。）
They smiled and clapped at the end.（最後に二人は笑って拍手しました。）
His little sister sat beside him and listened.（妹が横に座って聞いていました。）
Question: What did Ren do after practicing?（レンは練習のあと何をしましたか？）

練習のあとはオンラインで演奏した、なので 4 が正解です。
2時間練習した、は前の行動です。
