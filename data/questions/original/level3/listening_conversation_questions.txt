No.1:
W: My bicycle is ready at the shop by the river.
M: Great. Let's pick it up after club.
W: If it's closed, we can go on Sunday morning.
M: OK. I'll meet you at four.

Question No.1:
Where will they go after club?

1. To the bicycle shop by the river.
2. To the gym.
3. To Dad's office.
4. To a flower shop.

【正解1】
1. To the bicycle shop by the river.

【解説1】
放送文
W: My bicycle is ready at the shop by the river.（自転車が、川のそばの店で直ってるよ。）
M: Great. Let's pick it up after club.（いいね。部活のあと取りに行こう。）
W: If it's closed, we can go on Sunday morning.（閉まっていたら、日曜の朝でも行けるよ。）
M: OK. I'll meet you at four.（うん。4時に会おう。）
Question: Where will they go after club?（2人は部活のあとどこへ行きますか？）

女の子が「the shop by the river（川のそばの店）」へ行こうと言っているので、1が正解です。
日曜は店が閉まっていたときの予定です。4時は集合時刻です。

No.3:
W: Why are you wearing boots today?
M: Because we'll walk through the wet grass in the park.
W: Oh, right. The nature club meeting is there.
M: Yes. If it rains harder, we'll move to the science room.

Question No.3:
Why is the boy wearing boots?

1. Because he will walk through wet grass.
2. Because it is snowing at school.
3. Because his shoes are broken.
4. Because he has a P.E. class.

【正解3】
1. Because he will walk through wet grass.

【解説3】
放送文
W: Why are you wearing boots today?（きょう、どうしてブーツをはいてるの？）
M: Because we'll walk through the wet grass in the park.（公園のぬれた草の中を歩くから。）
W: Oh, right. The nature club meeting is there.（そうか。自然部の集まりがそこだね。）
M: Yes. If it rains harder, we'll move to the science room.（うん。雨が強くなったら理科室に移るよ。）
Question: Why is the boy wearing boots?（男の子がブーツをはいている理由は何ですか？）

男の子が「wet grass」を歩くと言っているので、1が正解です。
理科室は雨が強くなったときの変更で、いまブーツをはく理由ではありません。

No.6:
M: How long have you played the violin?
W: Since I was eight years old.
M: That's a long time. Do you still take lessons?
W: Yes. I have a lesson every Saturday morning.

Question No.6:
How long has the girl played the violin?

1. Since she was eight years old.
2. For eight minutes.
3. Only on Saturday morning.
4. Until last year.

【正解6】
1. Since she was eight years old.

【解説6】
放送文
M: How long have you played the violin?（バイオリンはどのくらい弾いているの？）
W: Since I was eight years old.（8歳のときから。）
M: That's a long time. Do you still take lessons?（長いね。いまでもレッスンある？）

Question: How long has the girl played the violin?（女の子はどのくらいバイオリンを弾いていますか？）

「How long」に対して開始時点の「Since I was eight」が正解です。1が正解です。
土曜の朝はレッスンの曜日で、期間の長さそのものではありません。

No.8:
M: I'm nervous about my speech tomorrow.
W: Don't worry. You've practiced a lot.
M: Still, I keep thinking about mistakes.
W: If you speak slowly, you'll be fine.

Question No.8:
Why does the girl tell him not to worry?

1. Because the speech is about animals.
2. Because he has practiced a lot.
3. Because tomorrow is Tuesday.
4. Because she bought a new notebook.

【正解8】
2. Because he has practiced a lot.

【解説8】
放送文
M: I'm nervous about my speech tomorrow.（あしたのスピーチが不安だよ。）
W: Don't worry. You've practiced a lot.（心配しないで。たくさん練習したでしょ。）

Question: Why does the girl tell him not to worry?（女の子が「心配しないで」と言う理由は何ですか？）

「You've practiced a lot」が理由なので、2が正解です。
ゆっくり話す、は追加のアドバイスです。スピーチの題や曜日の話はありません。
