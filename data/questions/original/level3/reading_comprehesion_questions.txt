本文1
Rocket Workshop Night
The craft club will have a Rocket Workshop Night.

When: Friday, October 17, 6:00–8:00 p.m.
Where: Room 304

We will make simple rockets with paper and plastic bottles.
Please wear old clothes. Bring a notebook and a pencil.

If you want to join, talk to Ms. Aoki by October 10.

問題1a:
What will students make at Rocket Workshop Night?

選択肢1a:
1. Paper airplanes only.
2. Simple rockets with paper and plastic bottles.
3. A large wooden boat.
4. Plastic models of cars.

【正解1a】
2. Simple rockets with paper and plastic bottles.

【解説1a】
正解は「Simple rockets with paper and plastic bottles.」です。
本文に「We will make simple rockets with paper and plastic bottles.」とあります。木の船や車のプラモデルの話はありません。
ヒント：作るものは make のあとを読むと見つかります。

問題1b:
When should students talk to Ms. Aoki to join?

選択肢1b:
1. On October 17 only.
2. After Rocket Workshop Night.
3. By October 10.
4. Every Friday in October.

【正解1b】
3. By October 10.

【解説1b】
正解は「By October 10.」です。
本文に「talk to Ms. Aoki by October 10」とあります。10月17日は当日、毎週金曜という指定はありません。
ヒント：参加申し込みの期限は by / before のあとの日付です。

本文2
From: Haruto
To: Mia
Subject: Saturday hike

Hi Mia,

Are you free this Saturday? Our hiking club will walk up Pine Hill.
We will meet at West Station at 8:30 a.m.

Please wear strong shoes and bring water and a light lunch.
If it rains, we will visit the city museum instead.

Can you come? Send me a message by Thursday.

Haruto

問題2a:
Where will they meet if the weather is fine?

選択肢2a:
1. At the city museum.
2. At Pine Hill gate.
3. At West Station.
4. At Haruto's house.

【正解2a】
3. At West Station.

【解説2a】
正解は「At West Station.」です。
本文に「We will meet at West Station at 8:30 a.m.」とあります。博物館は雨のときの代わりの予定です。
ヒント：meet / instead の文を分けて読むと、天気ごとの場所がはっきりします。

問題2b:
What should Mia bring?

選択肢2b:
1. A heavy coat and an umbrella only.
2. Water and a light lunch.
3. A camera for the museum.
4. Tickets for the train.

【正解2b】
2. Water and a light lunch.

【解説2b】
正解は「Water and a light lunch.」です。
本文に「bring water and a light lunch」とあります。くつは「wear」で持参ではなく着用です。乗車券の指定はありません。
ヒント：bring（持っていく）と wear（着る）を区別すると選びやすいです。

本文3
Last Sunday, Rina found a small brown dog near the bus stop.
The dog looked cold and hungry, so she took it home.

Her father called the number on the dog's tag.
A woman named Ms. Kondo came in the evening.
She said the dog's name was Momo and thanked Rina many times.

Rina was happy because Momo could go home again.

問題3a:
Why did Rina take the dog home?

選択肢3a:
1. Because she wanted a new pet.
2. Because the dog looked cold and hungry.
3. Because Ms. Kondo asked her to.
4. Because her father found the dog first.

【正解3a】
2. Because the dog looked cold and hungry.

【解説3a】
正解は「Because the dog looked cold and hungry.」です。
本文に「The dog looked cold and hungry, so she took it home.」とあります。新しいペットがほしかったわけでも、最初から Kondo さんに頼まれたわけでもありません。
ヒント：so（だから）の前が理由です。

問題3b:
How did Rina's father find the owner?

選択肢3b:
1. He put a note on the bus stop.
2. He asked people at school.
3. He called the number on the dog's tag.
4. He waited at the station all day.

【正解3b】
3. He called the number on the dog's tag.

【解説3b】
正解は「He called the number on the dog's tag.」です。
本文に「Her father called the number on the dog's tag.」とあります。掲示や学校での聞き取りの記述はありません。
ヒント：owner（飼い主）を見つける方法は、tag / number / call などの語を探すと早いです。

本文5
From: Saki
To: Leo
Subject: Tent for camping

Hi Leo,

Can I borrow your family's tent next weekend?
My class will go camping by the lake.

I will pick it up on Friday after school and bring it back on Monday morning.
I will be careful with it.

If it is OK, please tell me where I should meet you.

Thanks,
Saki

問題5a:
Why does Saki want the tent?

選択肢5a:
1. Because she will go camping with her class.
2. Because she wants to sell it.
3. Because Leo asked her to keep it.
4. Because her class will study at the library.

【正解5a】
1. Because she will go camping with her class.

【解説5a】
正解は「Because she will go camping with her class.」です。
本文に「My class will go camping by the lake.」と、借りたい理由が書いてあります。売る・預かるだけの話ではありません。
ヒント：borrow（借りる）の直後や次の文に、使う目的が来ることが多いです。

問題5b:
When will Saki return the tent?

選択肢5b:
1. On Friday after school.
2. On Sunday night.
3. On Monday morning.
4. Next month.

【正解5b】
3. On Monday morning.

【解説5b】
正解は「On Monday morning.」です。
本文に「bring it back on Monday morning」とあります。金曜は受け取り、月曜が返却です。
ヒント：pick up（受け取る）と bring back（返す）の日付を並べて読むとまちがいません。
