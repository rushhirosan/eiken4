本文1
Ken's Lunch Notes

Ken started a notebook called Lunch Notes. Every weekday, he wrote the name of the main dish at the neighborhood cafe. His neighbors soon asked him to explain why some lunches were better than others. ( 1 ), Ken began to add a short comment under each dish name.

Ms. Ito, the cafe owner, read the notebook. She asked Ken to put one page on the cafe board each Tuesday. Customers could draw a star when they agreed with a comment. Customers drew the most stars on the days the cafe served warm rice. After a month, the cooks asked to see the stars. They did not change the whole menu. They ( 2 ).

Ken learned that simple notes can help other people when the notes are clear and regular.

問題1a:
( 1 ) に入れるのに最も適切なものを、1、2、3、4 の中から一つ選びなさい。

選択肢1a:
1. Before he bought a new notebook
2. Until Ms. Ito locked the cafe
3. Although the cafe was closed that week
4. Because his neighbors wanted more than a list of names

【正解1a】
4. Because his neighbors wanted more than a list of names

【解説1a】
直前の文は、近所の人が「なぜその昼食の方がよいのか説明してほしい」と頼んだ、です。( 1 ) はその理由で、Ken が料理名の下に短いコメントを書き始めたことにつながります。Because his neighbors wanted more than a list of names が合います。
1 Before he bought a new notebook は、ノートを買う前にコメントを書き始めたことになり、話の順と逆です。2 Until Ms. Ito locked the cafe は「店主がカフェを鍵で閉めるまで」で、コメントを書き始めた理由になりません。3 Although the cafe was closed that week は「カフェが閉まっていたけれども」で、本文にそんな週はありません。

【ポイント1a】
種別: 読解
見出し: Because のかたまり は直前の理由とつなぐ
・空所には1語ではなく、文のかたまりが入る
・Because のあとは、前の文で述べた理由になる
・逆の内容 ← Although。時の前後 ← Before / Until

問題1b:
( 2 ) に入れるのに最も適切なものを、1、2、3、4 の中から一つ選びなさい。

選択肢1b:
1. started serving warm rice more often
2. asked Ken to cook the Tuesday lunch
3. threw the notebook into the trash
4. closed the cafe on Mondays

【正解1b】
1. started serving warm rice more often

【解説1b】
調理員は星の数を見て、メニュー全体は変えませんでした。星が一番多かったのは、温かいご飯を出した日です。そのあとの They ( 2 ) は、変えなかったことと対になる、実際に始めたことです。温かいご飯をより頻繁に出した、がその流れです。最後の段落も、メモが人の役に立ったとまとめています。
2 は Ken に火曜の昼食を作らせた、とは書いてありません。3 はノートを捨てた、だと「メモが役に立った」という結びと逆です。4 は月曜にカフェを閉めた、とも書いてありません。did not change the whole menu のあとなので、全部をやめた選択肢は合いません。

【ポイント1b】
種別: 読解
見出し: did not のあとは、実際にしたこと
・星が多かった料理 ← warm rice
・A ではなく別の行動 ← They did not A. They ( ).
・空所のかたまりは、うしろのまとめの文とも矛盾しない
・本文にない行動（捨てる、閉めさせる）は入れない

本文2
From: Nao
To: Elena
Date: October 9
Subject: Friday star night

Hi Elena,

The science club will hold a star night this Friday. We will meet on the school roof at 7:00 if the sky is clear. Please do not go to the science room first. The roof gets cold after sunset, so bring a jacket. The teacher will bring two telescopes. You do not need your own, but you will share one with another student.

I also need to tell you something about the number of people. The roof is safe for only sixteen students. Your classmate Mio asked me yesterday if she could come with you. I would like to say yes, but the list already has sixteen names. Because she asked after the list was closed, there is no space left for her.

You can still come, and I hope you will. If Mio wants a place, she should wait to see whether someone cancels by Thursday noon. I will write to her if a space opens. If it rains, we will not use the roof. We will meet in the science room at the same time and watch a short video about the moon instead. We will finish by 8:30 either way.

Please tell me by Thursday morning if you can come. I have to give the teacher the final list before noon.

See you on Friday,
Nao

問題2a:
What is true about Friday's star night?

選択肢2a:
1. It will be held in the science room if the sky is clear.
2. The club has never held a star night before.
3. Elena must bring her own telescope.
4. More students wanted to come than the roof can hold.

【正解2a】
4. More students wanted to come than the roof can hold.

【解説2a】
屋上は16人までです。名簿はすでに16人で、Mio は締切のあとに来たいと言いました。だから、来たい人数が屋上の定員を超えています。
1 は逆です。晴れているときは屋上で、理科室に先に行かないようにと書いてあります。理科室は雨のときの場所です。3 も逆です。望遠鏡は先生が2台持ってくるので、Elena の自分の望遠鏡は不要です。2 の「初めて」とは書いてありません。

【ポイント2a】
種別: 読解
見出し: 定員と、希望した人数を比べる
・only sixteen と、あとに来た人をセットで読む
・晴れの場所と、雨の場所を入れ替えない
・本文に never となければ、「初めて」は選ばない

問題2b:
Why can't Mio join the star night?

選択肢2b:
1. She does not have a jacket.
2. The teacher thinks she is not ready.
3. She asked after all sixteen places were taken.
4. Nao canceled the star night on Thursday.

【正解2b】
3. She asked after all sixteen places were taken.

【解説2b】
Mio が来られない理由は、"Because she asked after the list was closed, there is no space left for her." です。名簿が16人で締まったあとに頼んだからです。
1 のジャケットは、Elena への持参の指示で、Mio が断られた理由ではありません。2 は先生が Mio の力不足だと言った、とは書いてありません。Nao は来てほしいと思っています。4 は中止ではありません。誰かが木曜の正午までに取り消せば、空いた席を Mio に知らせます。

【ポイント2b】
種別: 読解
見出し: Because のあとが、断りの理由
・asked after the list was closed が理由
・本文の別の指示（jacket）を、その人の理由にしない
・cancel = 「中止」ではなく、空席が出るかもしれない話

問題2c:
What does Nao want Elena to do?

選択肢2c:
1. Come on Friday and share a telescope.
2. Wait until someone cancels, and then decide.
3. Meet Mio in the science room at noon.
4. Bring a short video about the moon.

【正解2c】
1. Come on Friday and share a telescope.

【解説2c】
Elena への勧めは "You can still come, and I hope you will." です。望遠鏡は自分のを持たず、ほかの生徒と1台を共有します。
2 は Mio の動きです。席が空くのを待つのは Mio で、Elena ではありません。3 の正午は、木曜に最終名簿を先生へ渡す時刻と、Mio のキャンセル待ちの期限です。理科室で会う時刻ではありません。4 の月のビデオは、雨のときに先生側が見せるもので、Elena が持っていくものではありません。

【ポイント2c】
種別: 読解
見出し: 誰への指示かを分ける
・Elena への勧め ← You can still come
・Mio への案内 ← wait until someone cancels
・雨の代わりの予定を、晴れた日の持参物にしない

本文3
The Garden Bell

Last spring, neighbors at North Hill started a small garden behind the community hall. They did not start it because they wanted a quiet place to sit. They wanted fresh herbs for the Tuesday soup night. At first, only six neighbors stayed after work on Wednesdays to water the plants and pull weeds.

In June, the garden almost failed. The six neighbors watered the plants at different times, and on some days nobody came. The soil dried out, and several herb plants died. Mr. Okada, who lives next door, did not close the garden. He hung a small bell by the shed. A neighbor rang it once after watering. The next person, walking over from the hall, could hear the bell and know that the job was already finished. The bell was not a sign that the garden was closed.

By October, the garden was healthy again. The soup night was using the herbs in soup twice a week. It did not change the whole dinner menu. The neighbors also wrote each watering time on a card by the shed. New helpers could read the card and help without stopping Mr. Okada on the path every Wednesday.

The bell is still by the shed, but the neighbors use it less often now. A list on the hall door shows who will water the plants each Wednesday. Any neighbor can join if they bring a pair of gloves. Neighbors without gloves can still read the list, but they cannot work in the garden that day. Mr. Okada says the list, not the bell, is the way to check the plan.

問題3a:
Why did the neighbors start the garden?

選択肢3a:
1. To make a quiet place to sit behind the hall.
2. To grow herbs for the soup night.
3. To ring a bell for people in the hall.
4. To give gloves to new helpers.

【正解3a】
2. To grow herbs for the soup night.

【解説3a】
始めた理由は "They wanted fresh herbs for the Tuesday soup night." です。その直前で、座るための静かな場所が理由ではない、と否定しています。
1 は本文が否定している理由です。3 のベルは6月に、水やりがばらばらだったので後から付けたものです。4 の手袋は、今から作業する人の条件で、庭を始めた理由ではありません。

【ポイント3a】
種別: 読解
見出し: did not の次の文が本当の理由
・B が目的 ← They did not A. They wanted B.
・あとから出てきた道具（bell / gloves）は、最初の理由と限らない
・Why did they start なら、最初の段落を見る

問題3b:
Why did Mr. Okada hang a bell by the shed?

選択肢3b:
1. To show neighbors that the garden was closed.
2. To call the soup night to dinner.
3. So the next person would know the watering was finished.
4. To take the place of the list on the hall door.

【正解3b】
3. So the next person would know the watering was finished.

【解説3b】
6月に水やりの時間がばらばらで、来ない日もあり、ハーブが枯れました。Mr. Okada は庭を閉めず、小屋のそばにベルを掛けました。水をやった人が一度鳴らすと、次の人が「もう終わった」と分かります。
1 は本文の反対です。"The bell was not a sign that the garden was closed." とあります。2 の夕食への呼び出しとは書いてありません。4 も時期が違います。会館のドアの名簿は、今の確認方法です。6月のベルは、その名簿の代わりではありません。

【ポイント3b】
種別: 読解
見出し: その読み方を消す ← not a sign
・ベルの目的 ← could hear the bell and know
・本文が否定した意味（closed）は選ばない
・今の道具（list）を、前の季節の理由にしない

問題3c:
What is true about the garden in October?

選択肢3c:
1. The soup night used the herbs in soup, but not in every dish.
2. Nobody wrote the watering times down.
3. The garden stayed closed after the plants died.
4. Mr. Okada watered the plants by himself.

【正解3c】
1. The soup night used the herbs in soup, but not in every dish.

【解説3c】
10月には庭はまた元気になり、スープの会が週に2回、スープにハーブを使っていました。同時に "It did not change the whole dinner menu." なので、すべての料理に使ったわけではありません。
2 は逆です。近所の人は水やりの時刻を小屋のカードに書いています。3 も逆です。6月にいくつかのハーブは枯れましたが、Mr. Okada は庭を閉めていません。10月には was healthy again とあります。4 は Mr. Okada が一人で水やりをした、とは書いてありません。

【ポイント3c】
種別: 読解
見出し: その段落の事実 ← in October
・By October の段落が、その時点の事実
・did not change the whole menu = 「一部だけ変わった」
・June の失敗を、10月も続いていることとして読まない

問題3d:
How do neighbors check the Wednesday plan now?

選択肢3d:
1. They listen for the bell by the shed.
2. They stop Mr. Okada on the path every Wednesday.
3. They read a card by the shed.
4. They read the list on the hall door.

【正解3d】
4. They read the list on the hall door.

【解説3d】
最後の段落で、ベルはまだありますが、前ほど使いません。"Mr. Okada says the list, not the bell, is the way to check the plan." なので、今の確認方法は会館のドアの名簿です。
1 は以前のベルです。今の主な方法ではありません。2 は逆です。新しい人は、毎週道で Mr. Okada を呼び止めなくてよい、と10月の段落にあります。3 のカードは、10月に水やりの時刻を書いたものです。水曜の担当を見る今の方法は、ドアの名簿です。

【ポイント3d】
種別: 読解
見出し: 最後の段落の手段 ← now
・the list, not the bell で、今の方法が一つに決まる
・前の段落の道具（bell / card）が残っていても、今の確認方法とは限らない
・without stopping Mr. Okada = 「毎週先生に聞かなくてよい」

本文4
Shop Stories

The broadcast club visits one neighborhood shop each Monday for Shop Stories. In October, the bakery and the flower shop both asked for the same Monday. The bakery is noisy at noon, and the owner can talk only while packing bread. The flower shop is quiet after six, and the owner can walk through the shop with the microphone so listeners can see the work. The students could record only one shop that week. ( 1 ), they chose the flower shop.

Ms. Abe, the adviser, played that recording in the library at lunch. The club did not ask the school to announce the shop's address. A shoe shop then asked for Tuesday. Its counter is too noisy for listeners to see the work, and a Monday visit had to be quiet enough for that. The students wrote back and ( 2 ).

By winter, Shop Stories was still on the air each Monday. Listeners could picture the flower shop's work without going there first.

問題4a:
( 1 ) に入れるのに最も適切なものを、1、2、3、4 の中から一つ選びなさい。

選択肢4a:
1. Before either shop sent an invitation
2. Although both shops were closed that Monday
3. Because the flower shop was quiet and the owner could walk through it
4. Until the microphone battery ran out

【正解4a】
3. Because the flower shop was quiet and the owner could walk through it

【解説4a】
ベーカリーと花屋が同じ月曜を頼み、生徒はその週に1店しか録音できません。ベーカリーは正午にうるさく、店主はパンを包みながらしか話せません。花屋は6時すぎに静かで、店主はマイクを持って店内を歩けます。( 1 ) はその理由で、花屋を選んだことにつながります。Because the flower shop was quiet and the owner could walk through it が合います。
1 Before either shop sent an invitation は、招待の前に花屋を選んだことになり、話の順と逆です。2 Although both shops were closed that Monday は「その月曜に両店が閉まっていたけれども」で、両店はその月曜を頼んでいます。4 Until the microphone battery ran out は「マイクの電池が切れるまで」で、花屋を選んだ理由になりません。

【ポイント4a】
種別: 読解
見出し: Because は、録音できた店の条件とつなぐ
・花屋を選んだ理由 ← quiet after six と walk through
・その週に録音できるのは1店 ← only one shop
・順序が逆 ← Before。その月曜は両店が開いている ← Although
・Until = 「いつまで」で、選んだ理由にならない

問題4b:
( 2 ) に入れるのに最も適切なものを、1、2、3、4 の中から一つ選びなさい。

選択肢4b:
1. asked the school to announce the shop's address
2. asked the shoe shop to wait for a quiet Monday
3. told the shoe shop that Shop Stories had ended
4. agreed to record at the shoe shop's noisy counter on Tuesday

【正解4b】
2. asked the shoe shop to wait for a quiet Monday

【解説4b】
靴店は火曜を頼みましたが、カウンターはうるさく、聞き手は仕事を見られません。月曜の録音は、仕事が見える静けさが要ります。生徒は返事を書き、静かな月曜まで待つよう靴店に頼みます。asked the shoe shop to wait for a quiet Monday が合います。
1 は学校に店の場所を知らせる、で、クラブはそれを頼んでいません。3 は Shop Stories が終わったと伝える、ですが、冬も毎週月曜に番組は続いています。4 はうるさいカウンターで火曜に録音することに同意する、で、月曜の録音に必要な静けさと逆です。

【ポイント4b】
種別: 読解
見出し: 返事は、月曜に必要な静けさに合わせる
・うるさい火曜のカウンターでは仕事が見えない
・月曜の録音の条件 ← quiet enough for that
・学校への店の告知は、本文が否定している
・冬も毎週月曜にあるので、終了の返事は合わない

本文5
From: Sora
To: Mina
Date: June 3
Subject: A book behind the desk

Hi Mina,

I help at the used-book corner on Wednesday afternoons. It is not a shop. People leave books they have finished, and others may take one book home for free. I sort them by sticker color, not by the writer's name. Yellow means easy stories, blue means books about jobs, and green means books with maps.

Last Wednesday I found a blue book about bakery work, with photos of ovens. I did not take it home, because helpers may not keep a book on a day they are working. I wrote your name on a card and left the book behind the desk until Friday at noon. After that, it goes back on the open cart.

Please do not come on Wednesday. That is my work day, and friends must follow the same rule. Come on Thursday after school, and arrive by five, because the library closes at six. If you cannot come by Friday noon, tell me on Thursday. I will not mail the book. You must pick it up yourself.

If you also want a yellow story for your cousin, look on the open cart. I cannot hold two books. The card is only for the blue bakery book.

See you,
Sora

問題5a:
What is true about the used-book corner?

選択肢5a:
1. People may take a finished book home without paying.
2. Helpers may keep any book on the day they work.
3. Sora sorts the books by the writer's name.
4. The corner sells maps and job books.

【正解5a】
1. People may take a finished book home without paying.

【解説5a】
読み終えた本を置き、ほかの人は1冊を無料で家へ持って帰ってよい、とあります。The corner is not a shop. なので、売る場所ではありません。
2 は逆です。働いている日に本を取っておくことはできません。Sora がパンの本を持ち帰らなかった理由です。3 も逆です。色のシールで分け、作家の名前では分けません。4 は、緑が地図のある本、青が仕事の本、という意味で、その場で売っているのではありません。

【ポイント5a】
種別: 読解
見出し: 無料で持って帰る場所 ← not a shop
・for free と not a shop をセットで読む
・作家名ではない ← sort by sticker color
・働く日の係への禁止 ← helpers may not keep

問題5b:
Why didn't Sora take the bakery book home on Wednesday?

選択肢5b:
1. Mina told her to leave it on the cart.
2. The library was closed on Wednesday afternoon.
3. The book was a green one, with maps.
4. Helpers cannot keep a book on the day they work.

【正解5b】
4. Helpers cannot keep a book on the day they work.

【解説5b】
理由は "because helpers may not keep a book on a day they are working." です。水曜は Sora の勤務日なので、その日にパンの本を持ち帰ることはできません。
1 は、Mina がカートに置けと頼んだ、とは書いてありません。Sora が Mina のために机のうしろへ取っておいています。2 は逆です。水曜の午後に Sora はそこで手伝っています。3 も逆です。その本は仕事の本なので青です。緑は地図のある本です。

【ポイント5b】
種別: 読解
見出し: because のあとが、持ち帰らなかった理由
・on a day they are working が禁止の条件
・青は仕事の本、緑は地図の本
・持ち帰らずに机のうしろへ置くこと ← hold

問題5c:
What should Mina do if she wants the bakery book?

選択肢5c:
1. Come on Wednesday and take it while Sora is working.
2. Pick it up herself on Thursday, before Friday noon.
3. Ask Sora to mail it if she is late.
4. Have Sora hold a yellow book for her cousin at the same time.

【正解5c】
2. Pick it up herself on Thursday, before Friday noon.

【解説5c】
Mina への案内は、水曜は来ないこと、木曜の放課後がよいこと、自分で受け取ることです。取り置きは金曜の正午までです。
1 は、Please do not come on Wednesday. と逆です。勤務日のルールは、友だちも同じように守らなければなりません。3 は、I will not mail the book. とあるので、送ってもらうことはできません。4 は、I cannot hold two books. です。いとこ向けの黄色い本は、その日の開いたカートで自分で見ます。カードは青いパンの本だけです。

【ポイント5c】
種別: 読解
見出し: 誰の行動かを、禁止の文とセットで読む
・Mina への指示 ← do not come on Wednesday
・遅れても郵送しない ← will not mail
・黄色い本は対象外 ← cannot hold two books

本文6
Night Market Rules

Riverside School holds a night market every October so clubs can sell one homemade item. It is not a fund-raiser for the school office. Each club keeps its money for its own supplies. In the first year, selling lasted until nine. Neighbors wrote about noise. The principal did not cancel the market. She moved the closing time to seven thirty.

In the second year, clubs could still sell calendars, postcards, and small plants. They could not sell anything that needed a heater or a flame. The cooking club had planned hot soup. It switched to jars of dried herbs, packed at school the day before, not on the market night. A teacher at the gate checked bags. She did not taste the food. She only made sure no club brought a heater.

This year, rain means the gym, and clear weather means the field. The choice is made at noon. A white sign on the front door shows the place. A blue sign means canceled. That blue sign has been used only once, after a typhoon. Parents should read the sign before they park. The field gate opens at six, but cars must park on the street. The gym lot belongs to the city pool on Saturday nights.

A club that finishes early must fold its table and take it to the storage room. The principal says an empty table makes visitors think the club has gone home, even while the market is open. Money is counted in the storage room, not at the table. A folded table means that club has stopped selling. Other clubs may still sell until seven thirty.

問題6a:
Why did the closing time change after the first year?

選択肢6a:
1. The school office needed the clubs' money.
2. Neighbors complained about noise, and the market continued with an earlier end.
3. The cooking club needed more time to heat soup.
4. A typhoon forced the school to cancel every October market.

【正解6a】
2. Neighbors complained about noise, and the market continued with an earlier end.

【解説6a】

The family kept a notebook called the Window Log. Each afternoon, one person wrote the temperature by the hall window and whether the curtain was open. At first, the notes were only numbers. 【ポイント7a】
