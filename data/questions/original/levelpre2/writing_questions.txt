・最初に I think ... / I don't think ...
・理由は First, ... Second, ...
・日曜の静かな部屋の話題から外れない

■ 参考解答の例
I think towns should keep one quiet room open on Sundays. First, some students cannot study at home because the rooms are noisy. A public room gives them a table and a lamp. Second, the room can close in the late afternoon, so workers still have a rest. It does not need to stay open at night.

■ 表現のメモ
I think towns should ... / First, ... / Second, ... / in the late afternoon

【ポイント12】
種別: ライティング
見出し: 意見のあと理由を2つ
・最初の文で賛成か反対かを言う
・First と Second で理由を分ける
・目安は50〜60語
