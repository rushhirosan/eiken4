問題1:
【Title】
Riku's Dog

【Passage】
Riku is 8 years old. He has a dog. The dog's name is Pochi. Pochi is brown. Riku walks with Pochi every day.

【Questions】
1. How old is Riku?
2. What color is Pochi?
3. What animal do you like?

【参考解答】
■ この問題で求められること
約20語のパッセージを黙読→音読したあと、内容についての質問2問と、自分自身についての質問1問に答えます。

■ 音読のポイント
・一文ずつ区切ってはっきり読む
・Riku / Pochi などの名前も丁寧に

■ 参考解答の例
1. He is 8 years old.
2. He is brown. / Pochi is brown.
3. I like dogs. / I like cats. / I like birds.

■ 表現のメモ
内容質問は He / She / It に置き換えて答えると自然です。No.3 は自分の答えを短く言えばOKです。

問題2:
【Title】
Yui's School

【Passage】
Yui goes to school by bus. Her school is big. She has many friends. She likes music class.

【Questions】
1. How does Yui go to school?
2. What class does Yui like?
3. What subject do you like?

【参考解答】
■ この問題で求められること
パッセージを黙読・音読し、内容質問2問と自分についての質問1問に答えます。

■ 音読のポイント
・by bus / friends / music をはっきり読む
・文末まで落ちずに読む

■ 参考解答の例
1. She goes by bus. / By bus.
2. She likes music class. / Music.
3. I like English. / I like math. / I like PE.

■ 表現のメモ
乗り物は by bus / by bike / on foot などで答えられます。

問題3:
【Title】
Ken's Lunch

【Passage】
Ken eats lunch at school. He has rice and fish today. His friend gives him an apple. Ken is happy.

【Questions】
1. Where does Ken eat lunch?
2. What does his friend give him?
3. What do you eat for lunch?

【参考解答】
■ この問題で求められること
内容2問と自分のこと1問に、短い英文で答えます。

■ 音読のポイント
・lunch / rice / apple をはっきり
・今日のメニューの文を区切って読む

■ 参考解答の例
1. He eats lunch at school. / At school.
2. An apple. / His friend gives him an apple.
3. I eat rice. / I eat bread. / I eat noodles.

問題4:
【Title】
A Rainy Day

【Passage】
It is rainy today. Sora has a yellow umbrella. She walks to the store with her mother. They buy milk.

【Questions】
1. What color is Sora's umbrella?
2. What do they buy?
3. Do you like rainy days?

【参考解答】
■ この問題で求められること
天気と行動のパッセージです。内容2問のあと、自分の好みを答えます。

■ 音読のポイント
・rainy / umbrella / milk を丁寧に
・with her mother を続けて読む

■ 参考解答の例
1. It is yellow. / Yellow.
2. They buy milk. / Milk.
3. Yes, I do. / No, I don't. / Yes. I like rain.

問題5:
【Title】
My Grandfather

【Passage】
Hana's grandfather lives near the sea. He has a small boat. On Sunday, Hana visits him. They look at the boats.

【Questions】
1. Where does Hana's grandfather live?
2. When does Hana visit him?
3. Who do you like to visit?

【参考解答】
■ この問題で求められること
家族の話です。場所・時の内容質問のあと、自分の答えを言います。

■ 音読のポイント
・grandfather / sea / Sunday をはっきり
・near the sea をひとまとまりで

■ 参考解答の例
1. He lives near the sea. / Near the sea.
2. On Sunday. / She visits him on Sunday.
3. I visit my grandmother. / My uncle. / My friend.
