問題1:
【Title】
Lunch at School

【Passage】
Ken has lunch at school every day. He usually eats a sandwich and juice. Sometimes he talks with his friends. He likes lunch time.

【Illustration】
教室の昼食風景。男の子がサンドイッチを食べている。女の子はお弁当を開けている。もう一人の男の子はジュースを飲んでいる。

【Questions】
1. [passage] Where does Ken have lunch?
2. [passage] What does Ken usually eat?
3. [illustration] What is the boy eating?
4. [personal] What do you usually eat for lunch?

【参考解答】
■ この問題で求められること
4級スピーキングは、約25語のパッセージ音読のあと、内容質問2問・イラスト質問1問・自分のこと1問です。

■ 参考解答の例
1. He has lunch at school. / At school.
2. He usually eats a sandwich and juice. / A sandwich and juice.
3. He is eating a sandwich. / A sandwich.
4. I usually eat rice and miso soup. / I eat bread.

■ 表現のメモ
イラスト質問は現在進行形（He is -ing.）で答えると自然です。

【Passage】
Tom has a new red bike. He rides it to school every morning. His best friend rides with him. They enjoy riding together.

【Illustration】

【Questions】
1. [passage] What color is Tom's new bike?
2. [passage] Where does Tom ride his bike every morning?
3. [illustration] How many bikes are there?
4. [personal] Do you have a bike?

【参考解答】
■ 参考解答の例
1. It is red. / Red. / His bike is red.
2. He rides it to school. / To school.
3. There are two bikes. / Two.
4. Yes, I do. / No, I don't. / Yes. I have a blue bike.
