本文1
Summer Camp
There will be a camping trip for students in August.

When: August 12-14
Where: Ruess River Camp

Students will go fishing and learn about the trees and flowers around the river.

We will meet in front of the school and go to the camp by bus.

If you are interested, talk to Ms. Wilson before June 3.

問題1a:
What will students do at the camp?

選択肢1a:
1. Take pictures of the river.
2. Clean the bus.
3. Go fishing.
4. Climb trees.

【正解1a】
3. Go fishing.

【解説1a】
本文に「Students will go fishing and learn about the trees and flowers」とあり、指定の活動は釣りと自然観察です。写真・バス掃除・木登りの記述はありません。

問題1b:
Where will the students meet?

選択肢1b:
1. In front of the school.
2. In Ms. Wilson's room.
3. At the bus stop.
4. At the river.

【正解1b】
1. In front of the school.

【解説1b】
集合場所は案内に「We will meet in front of the school and go to the camp by bus」と明記されています。Wilson先生の部屋は相談先、バス停や川は本文の集合地として書かれていません。

本文3
Fun at the Beach Jill is from a big city in Canada. 

Every summer, she visits the beach with her family. She enjoys swimming there. 

One day last summer, she also went fishing with her father. 

They got up at six in the morning and got on a boat at seven. 

They went fishing for four hours, but they didn’t catch any fish. 

Jill didn’t like fishing. The next day, Jill went for a walk by the beach at noon. 

She met a woman with long hair there. Jill asked her, “Do you like fishing?” 

The woman said, “No, but I love surfing.” Jill wanted to try surfing. The woman said, “You should take surfing lessons first.” 

Last fall, Jill went to college. At college, she started taking surfing lessons. 

Her first lesson was in the college swimming pool. Now, Jill has a lesson every week. 

This summer, she will take a trip with her friends. They will go surfing in the sea.

問題3a:
What does Jill do every summer? 

選択肢3a:
1. She goes fishing. 
2. She goes to the swimming pool.
3. She goes to the beach.
4. She goes on a boat trip. 

【正解3a】
3. She goes to the beach.

【解説3a】
習慣として「Every summer, she visits the beach with her family. She enjoys swimming there」と毎年家族と海辺を訪れます。釣りはある夏の1日、プールのサーフィン練習やボート旅行は別の時期の話です。

問題3b:
What time did Jill and her father get on a boat? 

選択肢3b:
1. At four.
2. At six.
3. At seven.
4. At noon. 

【正解3b】
3. At seven.

【解説3b】
釣りに行った日は「They got up at six in the morning and got on a boat at seven」とあり、ボートに乗ったのは7時です。6時は起床、正午は翌日の散歩の時刻なので混同しないようにします。

問題3c:
Who did Jill meet by the beach? 

選択肢3c:
1. A man from a big city.
2. A woman with long hair.
3. Her father’s friend.
4. Her swimming coach. 

【正解3c】
2. A woman with long hair.

【解説3c】
海辺の散歩で「She met a woman with long hair there」と長い髪の女性に出会った、と描写されています。大都市の男性・父の友人・水泳コーチが登場する、という記述は本文にありません。

問題3d:
What did Jill do at college? 

選択肢3d:
1. She joined a fishing club.
2. She learned to swim.
3. She started taking surfing lessons.
4. She cleaned the pool. 

【正解3d】
3. She started taking surfing lessons.

【解説3d】
大学進学後「At college, she started taking surfing lessons」とサーフィンレッスンを始めました。釣りクラブ加入・水泳習得・プール掃除は、本文にない大学での活動です。

問題3e:
What will Jill do this summer? 

選択肢3e:
1. Buy a new boat.
2. Get a summer job. 
3. Have swimming lessons at college.
4. Take a trip with her friends.

【正解3e】
4. Take a trip with her friends.

【解説3e】
「This summer, she will take a trip with her friends. They will go surfing」と今年の夏の予定が書かれています。ボート購入やアルバイト・大学の泳ぎの授業は別の文脈です。

本文6
A Fun Trip

Wendy is very busy. She does homework every day, and she has many hobbies. She takes piano lessons on Wednesday evenings, and she plays baseball every Saturday. During vacations, she spends time with her parents.

Last summer, Wendy and her parents stayed on a farm in the mountains for eight days. It took five hours to get there by car. There were 600 sheep on the farm. Wendy and her parents took care of the sheep with the farmer.

During their trip, they woke up at seven o'clock. Every morning, Wendy and her mother gave food to the sheep. Wendy's father cooked breakfast for everyone.

When Wendy came home, she told her grandfather about it. She said, "Helping on the farm was fun!" She showed her grandfather some photos of the farm. Her grandfather put the photos in a photo album for her. Wendy likes looking at the photos because she really enjoyed her trip to the farm.


問題6a:
What does Wendy do every day?

選択肢6a:
1. She plays the piano.
2. She does her homework.
3. She plays baseball.
4. She watches sports with her parents.

【正解6a】
2. She does her homework.

【解説6a】
冒頭に「She does homework every day」と毎日の行動として書かれています。ピアノは水曜夜、野球は土曜、親とスポーツを見る時間は休暇中の話で「every day」の行動ではありません。

問題6b:
Where did Wendy and her parents go last summer?

選択肢6b:
1. To the mountains.
2. To a big city.
3. To her grandfather's house.
4. To a new restaurant.

【正解6b】
1. To the mountains.

【解説6b】
去年の夏「Wendy and her parents stayed on a farm in the mountains for eight days」と山間の農場に滞在しました。大都市・祖父の家・新しいレストランへ行った、という記述はありません。

問題6c:
What time did Wendy and her parents wake up during their trip?

選択肢6c:
1. At five o'clock.
2. At six o'clock.
3. At seven o'clock.
4. At eight o'clock.

【正解6c】
3. At seven o'clock.

【解説6c】
農場滞在中「During their trip, they woke up at seven o'clock」と起床時刻が書かれています。5時・6時・8時という選択肢の時刻は本文になく、7時が正解です。

問題6d:
What did Wendy and her mother do every morning during their trip?

選択肢6d:
1. They walked to a store.
2. They gave food to the sheep.
3. They cooked breakfast.
4. They talked to the farmer's children.

【正解6d】
2. They gave food to the sheep.

【解説6d】
毎朝「Wendy and her mother gave food to the sheep」と母娘が羊に餌をやり、父は「Wendy's father cooked breakfast for everyone」と朝食を作ります。母娘が朝食を作る・店へ歩く・農家の子と話す、とは書かれていません。


問題6e:
Why does Wendy like looking at the photos?

選択肢6e:
1. They were a present.
2. Her parents are in them.
3. She really enjoyed her trip.
4. Her grandfather took them.

【正解6e】
3. She really enjoyed her trip.

【解説6e】
Wendyは写真を見るのが好きな理由として「because she really enjoyed her trip to the farm」と農場旅行を楽しんだから、と述べています。祖父が撮影したから・プレゼントだから・両親が写っているから、という理由ではありません。

本文9  
The School Performance

Gerald enjoys playing the trumpet. He practices every day after school.  
His father plays the guitar in a band with a violin player and a pianist.  
The band plays at a nice restaurant every week. Gerald wants to become a musician like his father.

In May, Gerald saw a poster about a music performance for a school festival.  
The festival was in July. Gerald’s friend, Jessica, said,  
“You should play your trumpet at the festival!”  
Gerald decided to perform. In June, he practiced very hard.

At the festival, Gerald played one song. Many students and his parents were watching him.  
He was nervous, and he played the song too quickly.  
He thought, “I didn’t play well。」

However, the audience enjoyed Gerald’s performance.  
Jessica said, “Great job!”  
His father said, “You played so well!”  
Gerald was surprised. He became happy about his performance.  
After the performance, he went to a pizza restaurant with Jessica and his parents to celebrate.

問題9a:  
What does Gerald’s father play?

選択肢9a:  
1. The guitar.  
2. The trumpet.  
3. The violin.  
4. The piano.

【正解9a】  
1. The guitar.

【解説9a】  
「His father plays the guitar in a band with a violin player and a pianist」と父はギターを演奏します。トランペットはGerald自身、ヴァイオリンとピアノはバンドの別メンバーが担当です。

問題9b:

選択肢9b:  
1. See his favorite band.  
2. Play sports after school.

3. Become a musician.

【解説9b】  
「Gerald wants to become a musician like his father」と Gerald は父のように音楽家になりたいと述べています。バンド鑑賞・放課後のスポーツ・レストラン勤務が目標、とは書かれていません。

問題9c:  
When did Gerald see the poster?

選択肢9c:  
1. In May.  
2. In June.

【正解9c】

【解説9c】

問題9d:

選択肢9d:  
1. nervous.  
2. surprised.  
3. happy.  
4. sleepy.

問題9e:

2. He went to Jessica’s house.

本文15
A Visit to a History Museum

George is thirteen years old. Recently, his sister wanted to go to a history museum. So, his family went to the museum last Saturday. The museum was in an old building. It was eighty years old. In the museum, he saw many interesting things. The best part was an old classroom.

George walked into the classroom and saw an old blackboard. The blackboard was forty years old. He was surprised because the blackboard was green. He also saw desks and chairs. They were dark brown. Then, George saw some old history textbooks. He saw a lot of interesting things in the classroom. He liked looking at the textbooks most because they were so old.

The history museum was very fun. George’s favorite subjects in school were English and math before, but now his favorite is history. George wants to go to more museums with his sister.

問題15a:
Why did George’s family go to the museum last Saturday?

選択肢15a:
1. George’s father had four tickets.
2. George’s mother works there.
3. George’s sister wanted to go.
4. George likes history.

【正解15a】
3. George’s sister wanted to go.

【解説15a】
「his sister wanted to go to a history museum. So, his family went to the museum」とあり、妹が行きたがったのが理由です。父のチケット・母の勤務先・George自身の好みが理由、とは書かれていません。

問題15b:
How old was the blackboard?

選択肢15b:
1. Thirteen years old.
2. Twenty years old.
3. Forty years old.
4. Eighty years old.

【正解15b】
3. Forty years old.

【解説15b】
教室の黒板について「The blackboard was forty years old」と40年物と書かれています。George は13歳、建物全体は80年なので、これらの数字と混同しないようにします。

問題15c:
What color were the desks?

選択肢15c:
1. Black.
2. Green.
3. Light brown.
4. Dark brown.

【正解15c】
4. Dark brown.

【解説15c】
George が見た机と椅子は「They were dark brown」と暗い茶色と描写されています。黒板が green だった、という別の情報であり、机と椅子の色は黒・緑・薄茶ではなく dark brown です。

問題15d:
What did George like most in the classroom?

選択肢15d:
1. The textbooks.
2. The desks.
3. The map.
4. The blackboard.

【正解15d】
1. The textbooks.

【解説15d】
教室では教科書・机・黒板などを見ましたが、「He liked looking at the textbooks most because they were so old」と一番好きだったのは教科書です。地図の記述はありません。

問題15e:
Now, George’s favorite subject is

選択肢15e:
1. math.
2. English.
3. history.
4. music.

【正解15e】
3. history.

【解説15e】
博物館訪問前は「George's favorite subjects in school were English and math before, but now his favorite is history」とあり、今の好きな科目は歴史です。音楽は本文に出てきません。
