問題1:
【Title】
Sam's Pet

【Passage】
Sam is 10 years old, and he has a dog. The dog's name is Lisa. Lisa is black. Sam likes Lisa.

【Questions】
1. How old is Sam?
2. What color is Lisa?
3. What animal do you like?

【参考解答】
■ この問題で求められること
公式の5級スピーキングと同じ流れです。約20語のパッセージを黙読→音読したあと、内容についての質問2問と、自分自身についての質問1問に答えます。

■ 音読のポイント
・はっきりした声で、一文ずつ区切って読む
・Lisa / Sam など固有名詞も丁寧に

■ 参考解答の例
1. He is 10 years old.
2. She is black. / Lisa is black.
3. I like dogs. / I like cats. / I like lions.

■ 表現のメモ
内容質問は主語を He / She / It に置き換えて答えると自然です。No.3 は自分の好きな動物を短く答えればOKです。

問題2:
【Title】
My School

【Passage】
Ken goes to school every day. His school is near his house. He has many friends. He likes English class.

【Questions】
1. Where is Ken's school?
2. What class does Ken like?
3. What subject do you like?

【参考解答】
■ この問題で求められること
パッセージを黙読・音読し、内容質問2問と自分についての質問1問に答えます。

■ 音読のポイント
・every day / near / friends をはっきり読む
・文末まで落ちずに読む

■ 参考解答の例
1. It is near his house. / Near his house.
2. He likes English class. / English class.
3. I like math. / I like science. / I like English.

■ 表現のメモ
Where には場所、What class / subject には教科名で答えます。

問題3:
【Title】
A Big Park

【Passage】
There is a big park in this town. Many children play soccer there. Some children read books. The park is fun.

【Questions】
1. Where do many children play soccer?
2. What do some children do?
3. Do you like parks?

【参考解答】
■ この問題で求められること
場所や行動をパッセージから拾って答え、最後は自分の好みを答えます。

■ 音読のポイント
・There is / Many children / Some children のリズム
・soccer / books / fun をはっきり

■ 参考解答の例
1. They play soccer in the park. / In the park. / In this town's park.
2. They read books. / Some children read books.
3. Yes, I do. / Yes. I like parks. / No, I don't.

■ 表現のメモ
Yes / No 質問には Yes, I do. / No, I don't. が基本です。

問題4:
【Title】
Tom's Family

【Passage】
Tom has a small family. He lives with his mother and father. His mother is a cook. His father is a teacher.

【Questions】
1. Who does Tom live with?
2. What is Tom's father?
3. How many people are in your family?

【参考解答】
■ この問題で求められること
家族に関するパッセージの内容質問と、自分の家族についての質問です。

■ 音読のポイント
・mother / father / cook / teacher をはっきり
・with his mother and father を一息で

■ 参考解答の例
1. He lives with his mother and father. / His mother and father.
2. He is a teacher. / A teacher.
3. There are four people in my family. / Four. / Three people.

■ 表現のメモ
How many には数字、What is ...?（職業）には a teacher のように答えます。

問題5:
【Title】
Sunday Morning

【Passage】
Mika gets up early on Sunday. She eats bread and eggs. Then she watches TV. She likes Sunday mornings.

【Questions】
1. When does Mika get up early?
2. What does Mika eat?
3. What do you do on Sunday?

【参考解答】
■ この問題で求められること
曜日・食事・行動を読み取り、最後は自分の日曜日を話します。

■ 音読のポイント
・gets up / eats / watches の三人称単数
・Sunday mornings を丁寧に

■ 参考解答の例
1. She gets up early on Sunday. / On Sunday.
2. She eats bread and eggs. / Bread and eggs.
3. I play soccer. / I study English. / I watch TV.

■ 表現のメモ
What do you do ...? には動詞から始まる文で答えると自然です。

問題6:
【Title】
A Blue Bike

【Passage】
Yuki has a blue bike. She rides it to school. Her bike is new. She likes her blue bike very much.

【Questions】
1. What color is Yuki's bike?
2. Where does Yuki ride her bike?
3. Do you have a bike?

【参考解答】
■ この問題で求められること
色・行き先を答え、自分の自転車の有無を答えます。

■ 音読のポイント
・blue bike / rides / very much
・一文ごとに区切る

■ 参考解答の例
1. It is blue. / Blue. / Her bike is blue.
2. She rides it to school. / To school.
3. Yes, I do. / No, I don't. / Yes. I have a red bike.

■ 表現のメモ
色は It is blue.、行き先は To school. でも通じますが、できれば全文で。

問題7:
【Title】
Lunch Time

【Passage】
It is lunch time at school. Emi has a sandwich and milk. Her friend has rice and soup. They talk and laugh.

【Questions】
1. What does Emi have for lunch?
2. What does her friend have?
3. What do you like for lunch?

【参考解答】
■ この問題で求められること
二人の昼食の内容を区別して答え、自分の好きな昼食を話します。

■ 音読のポイント
・sandwich / milk / rice / soup
・talk and laugh まで元気に

■ 参考解答の例
1. She has a sandwich and milk. / A sandwich and milk.
2. He/She has rice and soup. / Rice and soup.
3. I like curry. / I like ramen. / I like bread and juice.

■ 表現のメモ
friend の性別が不明なときは Her friend has ... のまま答えてもOKです。

問題8:
【Title】
Rainy Day

【Passage】
It is rainy today. Sora stays home. He reads a comic book. He also plays with his cat.

【Questions】
1. How is the weather today?
2. What does Sora read?
3. What do you do on rainy days?

【参考解答】
■ この問題で求められること
天気と行動を読み取り、雨の日の自分の過ごし方を答えます。

■ 音読のポイント
・rainy / stays home / comic book / cat
・also を落とさない

■ 参考解答の例
1. It is rainy. / Rainy.
2. He reads a comic book. / A comic book.
3. I watch TV. / I sleep. / I play games at home.

■ 表現のメモ
How is the weather? には It is rainy / sunny / cloudy. が定番です。
